Privacy Policy

Marmo Focus · Arda Velioğlu · Effective November 11, 2026

Marmo Focus is a focus timer that works entirely on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it stays. In short: we do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.

Who we are

Marmo Focus is developed and published by Arda Velioğlu ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact ardavelioglu.apps@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts on a server, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking or SDKs. We never receive information about you or how you use the app.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your device: the optional profile name you enter, your marmoset's name, your focus sessions (date, length and coins), the short notes you write after a session, your task list, Banana Coins, items and outfits, streaks and badges, and your settings.

This information never leaves your device through the app. If you use your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup), the app's data may be included in that backup under your device provider's terms.

"Create a Profile" only stores a name on this device so the app can greet you. It is not an online account and has no password or email address.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app schedules reminders (for example "session complete") locally on your device. They are not sent through any server and you can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your device settings.

Network access

The app works offline. It does not load fonts, images or scripts from other services. When it runs as a website, your browser naturally contacts the site that serves the app, and the app may read that site's current time to check the device clock; this request carries no personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

Because your data stays on your device, you are always in control of it. You can export a copy in Settings → Your Data → Export My Data, and erase everything in Settings → Your Data. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your device.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. As we hold no personal data about you, these requests are fulfilled by the tools above; you can still contact us with any question.

Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by your device's operating system and screen lock.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date, and describe significant changes in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: ardavelioglu.apps@gmail.com.
